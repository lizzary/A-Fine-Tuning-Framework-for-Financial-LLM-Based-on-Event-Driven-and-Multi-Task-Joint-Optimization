### Abstract
In the actual operation of financial markets, asset prices are not solely driven by smooth time series but are significantly influenced by sudden discrete events. Traditional time series models struggle to effectively utilize event-driven state transitions, while large language models, although capable of understanding event-related text, cannot directly generate trading strategies with statistical significance. To address this, this paper proposes a joint optimization fine-tuning framework. By constructing a structured event dataset through cumulative abnormal return modeling and integrating contrastive learning, ranking consistency loss, uncertainty-aware regression, and a preference alignment discriminator, the framework enables large language models to accurately map news text into event types, market direction, intensity, and cumulative abnormal returns. In backtests on CSI 300 constituent stocks from January 2022 to March 2026, the proposed fine-tuned model significantly outperforms general-purpose large models and time series models in terms of Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, and prediction accuracy, demonstrating superior generalization capabilities especially during sudden geopolitical events, thereby validating the effectiveness of the framework.
